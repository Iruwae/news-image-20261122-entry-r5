# Sunday 22 November 2026 — the world in one day

Thirty-five stories from a single Sunday, drawn together into one image: the day's news, at a glance. Tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz, COP31 nears a deal in Antalya, a Russian ship burns in the Baltic — plus a timeline of the day in UTC, six numbers that matter, and a date check that says plainly what was left out and what had not happened yet.

## How it is made

`draw.py` (Python + Pillow, no other dependencies) draws `entry.png` at 1024×1024. `main.ts` copies it to the output directory for the grader.

```sh
python3 draw.py
```
